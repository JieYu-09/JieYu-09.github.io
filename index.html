Research Experience

Opportunity Charging for Modular Autonomous Electric Buses

Oct 2024 – Present

Developed a joint optimization framework integrating vehicle scheduling, charging time windows, charger deployment, and battery-capacity decisions. The work exploits the coupling and decoupling capability of modular vehicles to create additional opportunity-charging windows without interrupting scheduled passenger service.

An earlier version of this work underwent first-round revision at Transportation Research Part D: Transport and Environment. Following reviewer feedback, the model formulation, experimental design, and manuscript presentation were further refined before the work was resubmitted to Journal of Energy Storage.

Detachment-Return Operations for Tidal Urban Mobility

Sep 2025 – Present

Investigated the spatiotemporal mismatch between passenger demand and vehicle resources under tidal commuting patterns. Proposed detachment-return operations in which redundant vehicle modules can be released at intermediate stops and reassigned to return-direction services, enabling module-level capacity redistribution across directions and corridor segments.

Built a joint scheduling framework covering initial vehicle composition, intermediate detachment, return services, subsequent task connections, infrastructure configuration, and passenger-stranding penalties.
