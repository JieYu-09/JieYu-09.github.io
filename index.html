Research Experience

Opportunity Charging for Modular Autonomous Electric Buses

Oct 2024 – Sep 2026

Developed a joint optimization framework integrating vehicle scheduling, charging time windows, charger deployment, and battery-capacity decisions. The work exploits modular coupling and decoupling to create additional opportunity-charging windows without interrupting scheduled passenger service.

Detachment-Return Operations for Tidal Urban Mobility

Sep 2025 – Present

Investigated the spatiotemporal mismatch between passenger demand and vehicle resources under tidal commuting patterns. Proposed detachment-return operations in which redundant vehicle modules can be released at intermediate stops and reassigned to opposite-direction return services, enabling module-level capacity redistribution across directions and corridor segments.

Built a joint scheduling framework covering initial vehicle composition, intermediate detachment, return services, subsequent task connections, infrastructure configuration, and passenger stranding penalties.
